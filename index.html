Real-World Case Studies & Educational Reference

Common Scams & Threat Anatomy

Examine six prevailing scam modalities prevalent across India, their observable red flags, and safe verification habits.

01. Fake KYC Update or Expiry

"Dear SBI Customer, your YONO NetBanking KYC has expired. Your debit card will be blocked today. Update PAN & Aadhaar immediately at: http://192.168.0.4/sbi-kyc"
Red Flags

Arbitrary same-day deadline, unauthenticated IP address link or lookalike domain, demand for identity and Aadhaar credentials via unverified HTTP link.

What to Do

Never click links in SMS. Indian banks explicitly state they will never send links asking you to update KYC or enter card PINs. Use only the official bank app or visit your physical branch.

02. OTP and UPI Collect Request Fraud

"OLX Buyer: I sent ₹15,000 for your sofa. Scan this QR code or accept the PhonePe collect request and enter your 6-digit UPI PIN to receive money in your account."
Red Flags

Claiming that entering your secret UPI PIN or scanning a merchant QR code is necessary to *receive* funds.

What to Do

Golden Rule of UPI: Entering a UPI PIN or scanning a QR code ALWAYS debits money from your bank account. Receiving funds requires zero authentication from the receiver. Reject immediately.

03. Parcel & Customs Duty Scam

"IndiaPost: Package #IN9821734 detained at Mumbai customs clearance due to unpaid duty fee of ₹48. Pay within 12h to prevent return: http://indiapost-customs-pay.top"
Red Flags

Abused top-level domain (.top), tiny token fee (₹48) designed to harvest full credit/debit card details and CVV on a cloned payment gateway, countdown pressure.

What to Do

Track parcels only on the genuine government portal (indiapost.gov.in). Postal authorities never demand payment via unofficial domains.

04. Fake Work-From-Home / Part-Time Tasks

"Earn ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 daily from home! Like YouTube videos and write hotel reviews on Google Maps. No experience needed. Contact HR Anjali on Telegram: @hr_task_pay"
Red Flags

Extravagant payouts for effortless actions, anonymous Telegram routing, initial small token payouts followed by demands for "prepaid recharge deposits" to release funds.

What to Do

Legitimate multinational enterprises never recruit through anonymous Telegram handles or require candidates to deposit funds to unlock tasks. Discard immediately.

05. Guaranteed Investment Schemes

"VIP WhatsApp Trading Club: 100% guaranteed return in 48 hours! Deposit ₹20,000 to get ₹60,000 profit. SEBI registered automated crypto algorithmic pool."
Red Flags

Promises of zero-risk guaranteed profits, unverified SEBI claims, funds collected into private personal savings accounts or obscure crypto wallets.

What to Do

Financial market investments inherently carry volatility; guaranteed returns are illegal and mathematically impossible. Verify registered brokers exclusively on sebi.gov.in.

06. Prize & Lottery Claims (KBC Hoax)

"Congratulations! Your mobile number won ₹25,00,000 in KBC All India WhatsApp Lucky Draw. Transfer ₹2,500 government processing GST to receive cheque."
Red Flags

Winning a contest you never entered or purchased tickets for, advance-fee demands for GST/clearance, transfer demanded via personal UPI ID.

What to Do

You cannot win a draw you did not participate in. Legitimate prize distributions never request winners to remit advance fees. Block the sender.

Safer Link & Communication Habits

Inspect the Root Domain, Not the Subdomain

In sbi.bank.com.scam-domain.xyz/login, the real destination server is scam-domain.xyz. Always identify the domain immediately before the first single forward slash.

HTTPS Is Not an Endorsement of Safety

The padlock icon only indicates the connection is encrypted; it does not verify the moral character of the server operator. Over 80% of modern phishing sites utilize valid SSL certificates.

Type Known URLs Directly

Whenever you receive an alert regarding banking, taxes, courier delivery, or telecom services, open a new browser tab and type the verified official address manually.

Expand Obfuscated Shorteners

URL shorteners (such as bit.ly or tinyurl.com) intentionally obscure destination domains. Use native URL previewers or append + to inspect the true endpoint.

If It Already Happened: Immediate Incident Response

If you inadvertently entered credentials, shared an OTP, or approved a fraudulent transaction, act within minutes:

Step 01 · Immediate Containment

Freeze Banking & Cards

Immediately contact your bank's 24x7 toll-free fraud helpline to freeze compromised debit/credit cards, disable net banking access, and de-link UPI IDs.

Step 02 · Identity Isolation

Rotate Passwords

From a clean, uncompromised device, change passwords on your primary email, banking portal, and mobile provider account. Terminate active sessions everywhere.

Step 03 · Statutory Reporting

Lodge Cybercrime Report

Report the incident to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in or dial national helpline 1930 immediately.

Important Verification Advisory:
Always verify helpline telephone numbers and official portal addresses directly on the official government website (cybercrime.gov.in) or your bank's physical passbook, never from search engine advertisement links.

Academic Research Project · AI & Cybersecurity

PhishGuard Architecture & Methodology

An explainable threat analysis paradigm designed for zero-latency client-side screening without server-side tracking.

Project Domain: Artificial Intelligence + Cybersecurity
Project Type: Undergraduate Capstone / Academic Research
Runtime Model: 100% Client-Side Engine (Zero Network Requests)

Abstract

Traditional anti-phishing solutions predominantly rely on centralized blacklist feeds (such as Google Safe Browsing or Spamhaus) or complex server-side deep learning classifiers. While valuable, blacklists exhibit notable latency windows (often several hours to days) during which novel zero-day phishing campaigns remain uncataloged. Conversely, opaque deep neural networks provide binary verdicts without providing human-interpretable rationale, leaving non-technical users unaware of the specific manipulation techniques employed against them.

PhishGuard proposes an explainable threat analysis architecture executing entirely within client-side browser runtime memory. By combining structural URL decomposition, multi-factor lexical tokenization, and transparent rule-based weight accumulation, the system delivers immediate, zero-latency risk evaluation while presenting users with verbatim textual evidence, heuristic weights, and concrete remediation actions.

Project Objectives

  • Zero-Latency Edge Execution: Perform all parsing and risk scoring within client browser execution memory, eliminating external network dependencies, privacy leaks, and latency.
  • Explainable Evidence Attribution: Ensure every triggered indicator is paired with verbatim matched text from the input, providing transparent proof rather than ungrounded claims.
  • Educational User Empowerment: Demystify deceptive social engineering patterns (such as UPI collect confusion, fake KYC deadlines, and authority "@" URL tricks) to foster proactive threat awareness.
  • Strict Privacy Guarantees: Guarantee zero persistence of user input data: no cookies, no localStorage, and no telemetry tracking.

Machine Learning: What Is and Is Not Here

In the preliminary research phase of this college project, we prototyped supervised machine learning classifiers (evaluating Logistic Regression versus Random Forest algorithms) trained on 15 extracted URL lexical features (including Shannon character entropy, path depth, token counts, digit ratios, and subdomain nesting).

While those models achieved high nominal validation accuracy (~96.4%) in laboratory evaluations on a small synthetic benchmark dataset, academic benchmark metrics fail to generalize reliably to real-world zero-day phishing campaigns. Synthetic datasets suffer from severe distribution shift, concept drift, and vulnerability to simple adversarial perturbations (such as character leetspeak or query padding).

Consequently, this public demonstration website intentionally contains NO server-side machine learning model and NO external reputation lookups. Instead, it relies purely on the verified, transparent heuristic rules engine detailed below. We deliberately avoid black-box probabilistic scoring claims in favor of explainable, verifiable engineering.

System Limitations

  • Static Lexical Boundary: The engine inspects lexical syntax and text patterns. It cannot execute remote client-side JavaScript or observe dynamic page rendering without fetching the URL (which would pose security risks).
  • Heuristic Evasion: Sophisticated adversaries utilizing novel non-standard phrasing or fragmented image-only text without OCR may evade pure lexical regex matching.
  • Context Blindness: The system cannot independently verify whether an email sender was authorized internally by an organization without performing cryptographic SPF/DKIM verification.

Future Enhancements

  • Client-Side WebAssembly ML: Embedding a quantized ONNX model running entirely in WebAssembly via Web Workers to combine zero-network privacy with generalized feature classification.
  • Visual Homoglyph Levenshtein Mapping: Implementing Unicode confusable matrix distance to flag visual lookalikes of top 1,000 corporate domains.
  • Cryptographic Bloom Filters: Distributing compact, privacy-preserving cryptographic hashes of known malicious domains for offline lookup without leaking queries.
